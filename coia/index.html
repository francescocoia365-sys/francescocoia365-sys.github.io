Between the orchestra and the machine

Francesco Coia is an Italian composer, arranger, orchestrator and sound designer based in Rome. He writes for film, television, theatre, dance and video games, moving between orchestral writing, electronic production and experimental sound.

He works alongside Paolo Buonvino in Rome and Aska Matsumiya in Paris, on feature films, a Netflix series, nationally broadcast documentaries and large-scale theatre. Projects he has contributed to have been recognised at the Nastri d’Argento and Globi d’Oro and presented at the Venice International Film Festival.

He holds a Bachelor’s in Jazz from the Conservatory of Pescara and a Master’s in Applied Music from Saint Louis College of Music in Rome, both with highest honours.
